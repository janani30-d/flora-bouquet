FLOWER CARE TIPS

Keep Your Flowers Fresh & Beautiful

Follow these simple care tips to enjoy fresh, vibrant blooms for longer and keep your floral arrangements looking their best.

Change Water

Replace the vase water every two days and keep it clean to maintain fresh.

Trim The Stems

Cut flower stems at a 45° angle every few days to improve water & freash

Avoid Sunlight

Keep bouquets in a cool place away from direct sunlight and heat sources.

Use Flower Food

Add flower food to the water to nourish blooms and extend their natural beauty.
