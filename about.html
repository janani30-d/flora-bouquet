FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our flowers, delivery services, and custom floral arrangements.

Do you offer same-day flower delivery?

Yes! We provide same-day flower delivery for selected locations when orders are placed before our daily cut-off time.

Can I customize my bouquet?

Absolutely! Our florists can create personalized bouquets based on your favorite flowers, colors, and special occasions.

Do you provide wedding floral decorations?

Yes, we specialize in wedding bouquets, venue decorations, stage florals, and complete event floral styling.

How do I keep my flowers fresh?

Trim the stems regularly, change the water every two days, and keep the flowers away from direct sunlight and heat.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and other secure online payment methods.
